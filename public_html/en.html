Questions You Probably Have

How do commission splits actually work?

You decide when you list the property. Agents do 50/50, so you both know the terms before any buyer walks through the door. No surprises. No negotiating after the fact.

What if I join but no one else in my area does?

We're launching region by region, starting with major markets. We're actively recruiting agents in your area right now. If after 90 days there aren't enough agents to make it valuable for you, we will give you another 3 months free. You risk nothing. But every network starts at zero. The agents who join early get the biggest advantage.

Is this legal? Are there any regulatory issues?

Yes, it's completely legal. Shared commission between agents has existed for decades — we're just making it efficient. Every agent must be properly licensed, and all transactions follow standard French real estate law.

What happens to my listings if I cancel?

They're immediately removed from the network. You can export your data before cancelling if you want to keep records. No lock-in. No penalties. You're in control.

How do you verify that agents are legitimate?

Every agent must provide their professional license number, agency affiliation (if applicable), and professional credentials before accessing the platform. We verify these with official registries. Agents who violate professional conduct or co-brokerage etiquette are removed immediately.

Can I try it without committing?

Yes. That's exactly what the 90-day free trial is for. No money taken from credit card. List your properties, search the network, see if it delivers value. If it doesn't, walk away. If it does, you'll wonder how you ever worked without it.
