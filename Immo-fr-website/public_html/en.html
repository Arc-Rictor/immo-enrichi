FAQ

How much does the subscription cost?

The subscription for agents costs €365 per year. It allows unlimited property listings, as well as access to searches on the platform.

How do I sign up?

To sign up, simply click on the "Create an account" button. You will be asked to provide your details, then redirected to the payment page.

I am an agency with hundreds of properties - how does the platform work?

Since agents have their own portfolio of properties, each agent registered as such can subscribe to the platform. If you are an agency without agents, it is up to the agency to subscribe.

Do I need to re-register every year?

No. Renewal is automatic each year. If you no longer wish to be subscribed, you must notify us 28 days before the renewal date.
